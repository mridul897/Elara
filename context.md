# Context: ELARA (women's fashion bags, Bangladesh)

Source: 8 AI-generated synthetic interviews plus published studies. Not validated with real users. Treat every line as a working assumption, never as a fact or a percentage. Overrides `principles/context-block.md` where they disagree.

1. **Who buys:** women 19 to 38 across Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet and Khulna, not only Dhaka. She rarely buys on first sight; she saves or screenshots a bag and returns two days later, so wishlist, save for later and recently viewed are core journeys.
2. **Where and on what:** mostly on a 390px phone, late evening (8 to 12pm) or on the commute; some buy expensive work bags on a laptop at a desk. Mobile is the floor, desktop is real for evaluating a high-priced bag. Some are on slow 4G, so page weight is a design constraint.
3. **How she arrives:** from Instagram, Facebook or TikTok, or a friend's recommendation, already holding one product in mind. Product pages are landing pages and must work without the homepage.
4. **What earns trust:** real customer photos, size in cm, material and interior detail, delivery charge and time shown before checkout, a human she can reach (WhatsApp or Messenger) who replies fast. Reply speed matters as much as policy text.
5. **What breaks trust:** colour that arrives darker than the photo, delivery charge revealed at the last step, vague return or exchange rules, AI-looking or stock imagery, no physical address or phone number.
6. **How she pays:** COD is the default for a new brand; bKash or card after trust is built; nobody pays in advance to an unfamiliar page. Keep COD, bKash, Nagad and card all visible; do not bury COD. Gateway and COD limit for high orders are still open with the client.
7. **Outside Dhaka:** the address form must have Division, District, Upazila/Thana, Area and an optional Postcode; she may not know her postcode. UI is English only for now.
